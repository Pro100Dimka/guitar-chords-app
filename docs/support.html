Where is the guide?

Open the guide tab in the app. Search for a question or feature name to find the relevant section.

The tuner or recorder cannot hear audio

Allow microphone access in your phone settings. Close other apps using the microphone and retry. Mention whether the issue follows backgrounding or locking the phone.

A song was identified but lyrics or chords are missing

Recognition and lookup use external services with different catalogs and availability. You can complete the text manually or import TXT/ChordPro. The app does not invent missing chords.

How do I preserve data before reinstalling?

Export a library backup in settings. Performance recordings are not included, so export important takes separately. Check the saved files before uninstalling.

How do I delete data?

Delete songs from the library and recordings from the take list. Uninstalling removes local app data. Email support to request deletion of correspondence. External services are described in the privacy policy.
